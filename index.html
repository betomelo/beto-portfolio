Between the

I'm Beto Melo, a multidisciplinary creative working at the intersection of design, photography, marketing, and data science. I'm currently studying Business Administration and Data Science at Northeastern University, with a concentration in Marketing and a minor in Design.

My interests don't fit neatly into one discipline, and that's what makes the work exciting to me. I'm interested in the relationship between how people think, what they create, and the systems that influence their decisions. Whether I'm approaching a problem creatively, analyzing a dataset, or developing a marketing strategy, I look for the stories that emerge when different perspectives intersect.

Photography gives me a way to explore identity, perception, and visual storytelling. Data science provides another way of examining the world, allowing me to discover patterns and communicate information through visualization. Marketing connects these interests through an understanding of audiences, behavior, and communication.

This website will be an evolving archive of ideas, experiments, and finished work. Rather than separating creative expression from analytical thinking, I want to explore how they can inform each other. My goal is to make work that encourages curiosity, challenges expectations, and finds meaning in unexpected connections.
